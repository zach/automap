# v1 ships start, extend, upgrade, adopt and eject

## Context

Day one covers start and one extend ([ADR-0010](0010-day-one-minimum-start-then-extend-once.md)); upgrade and adopt follow, in that order. Upgrade is where trust is earned or lost: in 2026 alone, Box2D 3.2 changed its callback signatures and Lua 5.5 changed `lua_newstate` ([02c](../research/02c-catalog-simulation-runtime-glue.md), [ADR-0002](0002-core-job-is-extend.md)). Adoption is what makes Automap useful to indies who already have an engine ([ADR-0003](0003-adopting-existing-engines-is-a-design-constraint.md)). Eject is the no-lock-in promise and costs little ([ADR-0040](0040-eject-is-one-cli-step.md)). Audit beyond license warnings ([ADR-0039](0039-licenses-are-facts-copyleft-warns-at-extend.md)) and porting to new platforms add new kinds of output and new platforms.

## Options

1. **Start and extend only.**
2. **Start, extend, upgrade, adopt and eject.**
3. **Every job, including audit and port.**

## Decision

Option 2. v1 ships five jobs: **start**, **extend**, **upgrade**, **adopt** and **eject**. Audit beyond the extend-time license warnings, and **port** (new platforms), come after v1.

## Consequences

- v1's vertical slices must cover all five jobs.
- Audit and port are deferred past v1.
